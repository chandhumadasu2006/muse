Our Story

Made for your everyday moments.

MUSE & MINT began with a simple idea: create a café that feels equally right for a quiet cup of coffee, a two-hour conversation, a creative work session or an unplanned evening with friends.

We roast with intention, bake every morning, and cook the kind of food that feels familiar but never boring — all served in a room designed to let the day slow down a little.

The Experience

Come for the coffee. Stay for the mood.

01

Slow Mornings

Early light, quiet playlists and the first pour of the day. Best enjoyed alone, with a book.

8 AM — 11 AM Bottomless filter refills on weekdays, fresh bakes from 7:30 AM, and the calmest seats in the house.

02

Work & Create

Fast Wi-Fi, plenty of plug points and tables built for long sessions.

Weekdays Dedicated work corners, unlimited water, and a quiet zone after 2 PM. Ask about our weekly work pass.

03

After Hours

Lower lights, a short cocktail list, and evenings that never really end on time.

After 7 PM Espresso-based cocktails, small plates, vinyl nights and the occasional live set on the terrace.

04

Weekend Brunch

Long tables, generous plates and the kind of mornings that turn into afternoons.

Sat & Sun Special brunch menu served 9 AM — 4 PM, with free-flow filter coffee and a live pastry counter.
